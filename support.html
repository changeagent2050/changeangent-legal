How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed entirely through Apple. In the app, go to Profile → Manage subscription, or directly via Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

I restored my phone / reinstalled the app — where's my data?

Sign back in with the same account. Your traits, progress, and streak are stored securely and will appear automatically. If something looks missing, try Profile → Restore purchases first, then contact us if the issue continues.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently and immediately removes your profile, traits, activity history, and streak data. This does not automatically cancel an active App Store subscription — cancel that separately through Apple if needed.

The microphone/speech feature isn't working

changeAgent uses on-device speech recognition during the "Declare" step. If it's unavailable, denied, or you'd rather not use it, tap "Tap to confirm instead" to continue without voice.
