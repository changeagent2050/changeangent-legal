changeAgent — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

changeAgent ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal-growth app that helps you build one trait at a time through small daily actions. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control or delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: the email address you sign up with, and an optional display name or avatar you choose to add.
  • Your activity in the app: the trait you're working on, the short text descriptions you write when you log a micro-action, and your progress (missions completed, streaks, tier).
  • Timezone: used only to determine your local day for daily limits and streaks.
  • Subscription status: whether you're in a free trial or have an active subscription, managed through Apple's App Store and RevenueCat. changeAgent never sees or stores your payment card details — Apple handles billing directly.

Speech recognition

During the "Declare" step, changeAgent can listen to you speak your declaration out loud using on-device speech recognition. This audio is processed entirely on your device and is never recorded, uploaded, or stored anywhere. Only the fact that your speech matched is saved.

How we use this information

We use your information solely to run the app: tracking your trait progress, enforcing daily limits, managing your subscription, and letting you pick up where you left off across sessions. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to operate the app, each bound by their own privacy and security practices:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles sign-in. Your account data and activity logs live here, protected so that only you can read or write your own rows.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status on our behalf.
  • Apple — processes your in-app purchase/subscription payment through the App Store.

We do not use any third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Your data, your control

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Profile → Delete Account). This permanently and immediately erases your profile, traits, activity history, and streak data from our systems.

Children's privacy

changeAgent is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

Data is transmitted using encrypted connections (TLS), and our database enforces row-level security so that each user can only ever access their own data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
